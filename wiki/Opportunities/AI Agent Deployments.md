# Overview

AI agent deployments offer commercial opportunities in automating complex workflows. Focus on sectors like ecommerce and health, where agents handle unstructured data and nuanced decisions with guardrails for safety.

# Key Insights

- **Ecommerce Opportunities**: Automate customer service (refunds, orders), fraud analysis, and vendor reviews. Multi-agent systems enable collaborative handling of inquiries.
- **Health Opportunities**: Process insurance claims, patient triage, and medical data interpretation. Guardrails ensure privacy and safety compliance.
- **Business Paths**: Enterprise B2B solutions for rule-heavy systems, with human-in-the-loop for high-risk actions. Potential for scalable agent platforms.
